Before your trip

Checklist for your arrival

  • Passport check at least six months of validity
  • eVisa apply online (50 USD, up to 90 days)
  • ZIC insurance take out (approx. 44 USD per person)
  • Travel health insurance with repatriation cover
  • Entry form: "Fumba Town" is enough as the address
  • Appointments confirm with our team beforehand
  • US dollars for arrival, the rest from an ATM on site

Details on entry and health are in the travel guide.
