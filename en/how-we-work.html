How we work

Who is behind this

Zanzibar Insider was started by people who live and work on Zanzibar, among others the team of FumbaTown Agency and Perspektive Ausland. We earn our money from consulting and property brokerage, not from this site. That is exactly why we state openly where there are connections.

The three labels

✓ Checked We have used the provider ourselves or checked it in person.

Unchecked A tip from the community or a public source. Sounds good, but we have not been able to confirm it ourselves yet.

Partner We work with the provider or the project belongs to our network. We say so.

Paid listings

There are no paid placements. Nobody can buy a Checked label. The order in the directory is: Checked first, then Partner, then Unchecked, each alphabetical.

How we check tips

Every submitted tip is read. We check whether the provider exists and whether the contact details are right. It then appears as Unchecked. It only becomes Checked once someone from the team or several independent readers have confirmed it. Providers who list themselves are marked as owner entries.

Errors and corrections

Prices, numbers and opening hours change quickly on Zanzibar. If you notice something wrong, tell us. We correct it as soon as possible and update the date.

No advice

All information without guarantee. On visas, taxes and legal matters we give an overview, not individual legal, tax or investment advice.
