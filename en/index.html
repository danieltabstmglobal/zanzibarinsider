How it works

  • You send a tip or a correction.
  • We check details and contact.
  • The entry appears as checked or unchecked with source and date.
  • Everyone can comment. Comments appear after approval.

Who's behind this?

Zanzibar Insider was started by people who live and work on Zanzibar themselves, among others the team of FumbaTown Agency and Perspektive Ausland. The site is open to everyone who knows the island: tips, corrections and first-hand reports are welcome. For a deeper dive into taxes and leaving your home country, see the Zanzibar country profile at Wohnsitz Ausland.
