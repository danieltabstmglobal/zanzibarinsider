Kibabu Cars

Our pick for a rental car. Travellers report that a car can often be arranged at short notice by email, with hand-over at the airport or port. Have vehicle, price and extra costs confirmed in writing beforehand.

Rule of thumb: One travel report mentions about 35 USD per day for an SUV plus about 10 USD for the temporary Tanzanian driving licence. Indicative only, please ask for current prices.

  • 📍 Zanzibar Airport / Stone Town
