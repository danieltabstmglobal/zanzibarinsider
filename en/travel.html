Coast & sea

The most beautiful beaches

Every beach has its own character, from lively to wild and romantic.

NungwiNorth coast, lively. Popular for sunsets, beach bars and diving.
KendwaNorth-west, relaxed. Wide beach, good for swimming, even at low tide.
PajeEast coast, kitesurfing. Africa's well-known kitesurf spot with schools for all levels.
MatemweNorth-east, romantic. Quiet beach, starting point for Mnemba Atoll.
JambianiSouth-east, authentic. Village feel, seaweed farms and calm.
FumbaSouth-west, calm. Beach club and lagoon at Fumba Town, close to the airport.
UNESCO World Heritage

Stone Town, the heart of the island

A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000. Winding alleys, carved doors, mosques and markets tell of centuries of trade between Arabia, India and East Africa.

  • Forodhani Night Market: seafood, Zanzibar pizza and fresh juices by the water
  • The old doors: Arabic and Indian carving as a landmark
  • House of Wonders: former sultan's palace
  • Spice Farm Tour: cloves, cinnamon and vanilla on the plantations nearby

Entry & insurance

Visa and mandatory insurance without stress

Filling in the tourist visa (eVisa): tips
  • You can apply online before the trip or on arrival at the airport. Online often saves 20 to 45 minutes at the counter in high season. Cost: 50 USD, valid up to 90 days.
  • Enter name, date of birth and passport number exactly as in the passport. The passport must be valid for at least six more months.
  • For the address of your accommodation the name of the accommodation or "Fumba Town" is enough. State your profession honestly, "self-employed" is fine.
  • Have a passport photo and a scan of the passport page ready. Print the confirmation or save it offline on your phone, the network at the airport is not always stable.
  • A photo is also taken at the counter. Forms change, in case of doubt what the official portal shows applies.

visa.immigration.go.tz

Taking out the mandatory insurance (ZIC): tips
  • Since 1 October 2024 the insurance is required for visitors to Zanzibar, about 44 USD per person, with family discounts. Takes a few minutes online.
  • The mandatory policy does not replace travel health insurance. Add a private policy with repatriation.
  • Have the confirmation ready on arrival, best printed.

visitzanzibar.go.tz

Health, money, SIM
  • No mandatory vaccinations. Recommended: hepatitis A and B, tetanus, diphtheria, typhoid. Yellow fever only when arriving from endemic areas.
  • Mosquito protection, bottled water only, sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, avoid the midday heat from 12 to 3 pm.
  • The currency is the Tanzanian shilling, US dollars are accepted in tourist areas. About 2,700 to 2,900 TZS per euro, the airport rate is often worse. ATMs in Stone Town and Fumba Town.
  • Local SIM (Vodacom, Airtel, Zantel) for about 25 USD including data, best bought right after baggage claim.
  • Culture: in towns cover shoulders and knees, ask before photographing people. "Mambo" and "Habari" are the standard greetings.
Getting around

Rental car, taxi and transfers

Traffic drives on the left. For a rental car you also need a temporary Tanzanian driving licence, which the rental company usually arranges for around 10 USD.

Kibabu Cars

Our pick for a rental car. Travellers report that a car can often be arranged at short notice by email, with hand-over at the airport or port. Have vehicle, price and extra costs confirmed in writing beforehand.

Rule of thumb: One travel report mentions about 35 USD per day for an SUV plus about 10 USD for the temporary Tanzanian driving licence. Indicative only, please ask for current prices.

Getting here

How to get to the island

There is no direct flight from German-speaking countries. The best connections run via Dubai, Istanbul, Doha or Nairobi.

Frankfurt

via Dubai, approx. 14 h

Munich

via Istanbul, approx. 11 h

Vienna

via Doha, approx. 12 h

Zurich

via Nairobi, approx. 10 h

15 to 25 min, taxi approx. 15 to 25 USD.

To Stone Town

10 to 15 min, taxi approx. 10 to 15 USD.

To Nungwi / Paje

45 to 90 min, taxi approx. 50 to 80 USD, rental car recommended.
